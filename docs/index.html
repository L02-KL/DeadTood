📚 DeadTood

Smart Academic Task Manager for HCMUT Students

🎯 The Problem

Students at HCMUT struggle with managing multiple assignments, quizzes, and project deadlines across different courses. Missing deadlines leads to stress, lower grades, and lost opportunities. Traditional calendars and spreadsheets don't provide the smart organization and timely reminders students need to stay on top of their academic workload.

✨ Core Features (MVP)

📝 Task & Deadline Tracking

Create and manage assignments, quizzes, and projects linked to specific courses. Never lose track of what's due.

📊 Dashboard Overview

Color-coded visual summary of upcoming tasks sorted by urgency. See what needs attention at a glance.

🔐 Basic Authentication

Secure login with Google or Email to protect your data and sync across devices.

🔔 Smart Reminders

Timely notifications and emails before deadlines to ensure you never miss important work.

📚 Course Management

Add, edit, and delete courses by subject code. Organize your entire academic term logically.

🚀 How It Works

1

Sign Up & Add Courses

Create your account and add all your courses for the semester using subject codes.

2

Add Tasks & Deadlines

Input assignments, quizzes, and projects with their due dates linked to specific courses.

3

Monitor Your Dashboard

View all upcoming tasks color-coded by urgency on your personalized dashboard.

4

Receive Smart Reminders

Get timely notifications before deadlines so you never miss an assignment.

👥 Built for HCMUT Students

DeadTood is specifically designed for students at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology who juggle multiple courses, assignments, and deadlines throughout the semester. Our MVP focuses on solving the core challenges of academic task management before expanding to advanced features.
